In-house Robotics Program

Learn Robotics. Build. Innovate.

📌 Overview

A continuous school robotics program designed to help children learn engineering fundamentals through practical robotics, mathematics, and science. The program can be conducted during regular school hours or as weekend classes based on the school's preference.

🎯 What Students Will Learn

Students will:

  • Build and program real-life robotic solutions.
  • Learn robotics fundamentals and basic engineering terminology.
  • Understand sensors, motors, and robotics experiments.
  • Learn NXT programming.
  • Build and program a MyBot capable of performing beginner and advanced tasks.
  • Study principles of light, sound, sensor technology, and mechanics.
  • Apply mathematics including addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, averages, and geometry concepts such as angles, triangles, and measurement.
  • Build robots including:
    • CastorBot
    • 3-Motor Chassis
    • HammerBot
    • Rattlesnake Robot
  • Design, build, and program their own robot.
  • Develop creativity, innovation, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
🏆 Program Challenge

The program concludes with a SumoBot Challenge and an Obstacle Challenge.

🤖 Robokidz Provides

  • Robotic Kits
  • Professional Trainers
  • Laptops
  • Programming Software
  • Level 1 & Level 2 Certificates upon successful completion
  • Study Materials
  • Writing Pads, Pencils/Pens, and Rulers
  • IHCP Hoarding for School Display

🏫 School Provides

  • Classroom or suitable space for conducting robotics sessions.

⏱️ Duration

5 Months (30 Hours) One 1.5-hour session per week.

Innovation Labs

Explore. Create. Innovate.

📌 Overview

The Innovation Lab provides students with access to advanced STEM technologies, allowing them to explore robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, and emerging technologies through practical, hands-on learning experiences. The lab encourages creativity, experimentation, and innovation while preparing students for future technologies.

🛠️ Innovation Lab Includes

  • STEM Robotics Kits: Comprehensive hardware kits for building interactive robotic solutions and learning mechanical assembly.
  • Artificial Intelligence Robots: Smart AI-enabled robots equipped with machine learning, vision recognition, and voice processing capabilities.
  • 3D Printers: State-of-the-art 3D printers allowing students to turn CAD designs into physical prototypes and custom components.
  • Drones: Programmable aerial drones for studying flight dynamics, sensors, autonomous navigation, and aerodynamics.
  • Science & Technology Kits: Experimental kits for physics, electronics, optics, and scientific exploration.
  • IoT, AR, VR, Automation & Control Kits: Advanced kits for Internet of Things, Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, microcontrollers, and automated sensor systems.

🎯 What Students Will Learn

Students will:

  • Explore Robotics and Artificial Intelligence.
  • Learn through hands-on experimentation.
  • Work with IoT, AR, VR, automation, and control systems.
  • Gain experience using 3D printers and drones.
  • Develop creativity, innovation, and real-world problem-solving skills.
  • Build future-ready technical skills through project-based learning.

✨ Additional Facilities

  • Learning Management System (LMS) with digitized learning content.
  • Wireless student response systems for interactive learning.
  • Online interaction with international experts and student communities.
  • Qualified trainers and expert guidance.
  • Funding support from Robokidz for establishing Innovation Labs in schools (where applicable).
  • Option to set up the Innovation Lab as a Build–Operate (BO) project for schools.

🏫 School Requirements

  • Wi-Fi-enabled room or dedicated space for establishing the Innovation Lab.

Afterschool Program

Advance. Create. Achieve.

📌 Overview

The Level A+ Program is an advanced robotics training program designed for students who are ready to move beyond the fundamentals. It enables schools to identify and nurture highly innovative students, helping them become future technology leaders through advanced robotics, engineering, programming, and problem-solving.

🎯 What Students Will Learn

Students will:

  • Gain in-depth knowledge of robotics and engineering concepts.
  • Learn advanced programming techniques.
  • Design and program increasingly complex robotic systems.
  • Build advanced robot models including:
    • 9797 Robot
    • Spikey Robot
    • AlphaRex Robot
  • Learn mathematical concepts including:
    • Fractions, Decimals, Percentages, Ratios, Proportions
  • Practice advanced programming concepts such as:
    • Colour Recognition, Switch Commands, Wiggler Programming
  • Design, build, and present their own robotics project.
  • Participate in SumoBot Challenge and Advanced Obstacle Challenge.
📱 Student Resources

Each student receives a dedicated learning tablet preloaded with Robotics Programming Software, Robot Design Software, Mathematics Learning App, and Robot Building Games to support independent learning and engineering knowledge.

🎁 Robokidz Provides

  • Robotic Kits
  • Professional Trainers
  • Laptops
  • One Tablet for Every Student
  • Programming Software
  • Level A+ Certificate upon successful completion
  • Creative Games for Logical Thinking
  • Alpha to Omega Mathematics Learning App
  • Coaching for National & International Tech Challenges
  • Study Materials
  • Writing Pads, Pens/Pencils & Rulers
  • LACP Hoarding for School Display
  • Gifted & Talented Technocrat Club Membership

🏫 School Provides

  • Classroom or dedicated space for conducting robotics sessions.

⏱️ Duration

10 Months (60 Hours) One 1.5-hour session every week.
