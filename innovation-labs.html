Innovation Labs

Explore. Create. Innovate.

An advanced innovation lab where students gain hands-on experience with robotics, Artificial Intelligence, IoT, drones, 3D printing, and emerging technologies while developing practical problem-solving and innovation skills.

📌 Program Overview

The Innovation Lab provides students with access to advanced STEM technologies, allowing them to explore robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, and emerging technologies through practical, hands-on learning experiences. The lab encourages creativity, experimentation, and innovation while preparing students for future technologies.

🛠️ Innovation Lab Includes

🤖

STEM Robotics Kits

Comprehensive hardware kits for building interactive robotic solutions and learning mechanical assembly.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence Robots

Smart AI-enabled robots equipped with machine learning, vision recognition, and voice processing capabilities.

🖨️

3D Printers

State-of-the-art 3D printers allowing students to turn CAD designs into physical prototypes and custom components.

🛸

Drones

Programmable aerial drones for studying flight dynamics, sensors, autonomous navigation, and aerodynamics.

🔬

Science & Technology Kits

Experimental kits for physics, electronics, optics, and scientific exploration.

🌐

IoT, AR, VR, Automation & Control Kits

Advanced kits for Internet of Things, Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, microcontrollers, and automated sensor systems.

🎯 Learning Outcomes

Students will:

✓ Explore Robotics and Artificial Intelligence.
✓ Learn through hands-on experimentation.
✓ Work with IoT, AR, VR, automation, and control systems.
✓ Gain experience using 3D printers and drones.
✓ Develop creativity, innovation, and real-world problem-solving skills.
✓ Build future-ready technical skills through project-based learning.

✨ Additional Facilities

The Innovation Lab also includes:

  • A Learning Management System (LMS) with digitized learning content.
  • Wireless student response systems for interactive learning.
  • Online interaction with international experts and student communities.
  • Qualified trainers and expert guidance.
  • Funding support from Robokidz for establishing Innovation Labs in schools (where applicable).
  • Option to set up the Innovation Lab as a Build–Operate (BO) project for schools.

🏫 School Requirements

The school should provide:

  • A Wi-Fi-enabled room or dedicated space for establishing the Innovation Lab.
